## The Inspiration
With hundreds of ed-tech platforms (Physics Wallah, Unacademy, Allen, CodeWithHarry, etc.) offering thousands of courses, students are overwhelmed. It's incredibly difficult to compare courses, read genuine reviews, and find the right fit for your budget and learning style. **LearnLens** solves this by bringing all top courses into one unified, searchable platform with authentic community reviews and an advanced AI assistant to guide you.

##  MLH Track: Best Use of Google Gemini
We integrated **Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash API** via Google AI Studio to power the **LearnLens AI Assistant**. 
By leveraging the advanced reasoning capabilities of the 2.5 model, students can open the floating AI chat and ask complex queries like:
* *"I have a budget of ₹5000 and want to learn Web Development. What do you recommend?"*
* *"Which is better for JEE prep: Physics Wallah or Allen?"*

The Gemini 2.5 AI acts as a highly intelligent, personalized educational counselor, making course discovery conversational, instant, and incredibly accurate.

##  Key Features
* ** Gemini 2.5 AI Counselor:** A real-time chat assistant powered by Google AI Studio to help students find the perfect course.
* ** Secure Authentication:** Seamless Email/Password and Google Sign-in powered by Firebase Auth.
* **Authentic Community Reviews:** A Reddit-style discussion and review section for every course so students get the real picture, not just marketing material.
* ** Unified Comparison:** Browse and filter courses from India's top platforms by price, rating, and category.
* ** Lightning Fast UI:** Built with React, Vite, and Tailwind CSS for a seamless, modern user experience.

##  Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** Typescript, Vite, Tailwind CSS
* **Backend & Database:** Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore
* **Artificial Intelligence:** Google Generative AI SDK (Gemini 2.5 Flash)
* **Hosting & Deployment:** Vercel
